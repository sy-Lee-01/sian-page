I'm Sian, an HCI researcher exploring how people and generative AI make things together.

I started in mechanical engineering, then moved into HCI because I wanted to work on problems that show up in people's real lives, and to enjoy the whole loop of finding them, building something, and testing it with people.

These days I build small AI tools to follow my own curiosity, and I study them by using them myself. I care about creativity support tools that people actually keep using, and about the gap between what research builds and what practice adopts.

I'm currently in Apple Developer Academy @ POSTECH as a 5th cohort learner. For our final challenge, my team is tackling the problems liver surgeons face in surgical planning, from UX to the technical solution.

I also like building silly but fun things :)
